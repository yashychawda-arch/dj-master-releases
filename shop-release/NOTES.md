A better request page for your guests.

- **Your night's name is the headline** on the booth link's page, the pill at the top says "Requests open" or "Requests closed", and the search button is in your theme colour.
- **Guests see how many requests they have left** (when you set a limit), and each of theirs as a chip: waiting for you, coming up, or not tonight. A request you turn down now shows "Not tonight" instead of vanishing, and gives the guest their place back.
- **"Asked for tonight" is numbered**, and songs slide up the list as guests back them.
- The fine print is one line at the bottom.

Windows: close DJ Master and run the Setup.exe, or install through the update banner. Mac: extract the ZIP and run Install.command. Account, activation, settings and music are kept.
