A new look, and extended intros.

- **Glass**: panels that catch the light, a floating side menu, and each section with the window to itself. Your theme colour carries through it all, and through your phone.
- **Dashboard**: the search box first, then one row of quick actions. With the booth link open it starts with the requests waiting on you, ready to play or turn down.
- **Extended intros**: on any track, make a copy that opens with 16 or 32 bars of the song's own music, vocal taken out, then runs into the song. The original is left alone. The first one downloads the vocal remover once (67 MB). The vocal remover is UVR-MDX-NET Inst HQ 3, trained by the Ultimate Vocal Remover project's developers, Anjok07 and aufr33 - credited in Settings > Help & legal > Credits.
- **Tidy names** in the crate: "Full Video: Ole Ole 2.0 - Jawaani Jaaneman | Saif Ali Khan" becomes "Ole Ole 2.0", here and in your DJ software's library. Your files aren't changed.
- Folders open as one searchable list, Insights shows your keys on the Camelot wheel, requests sit one to a line, and fixes from a working DJ's screen.

Windows: close DJ Master and run the Setup.exe, or install through the update banner. Mac: extract the ZIP and run Install.command. Account, activation, settings and music are kept. The installer now also installs onnxruntime (about 15 MB), which runs the vocal remover.
