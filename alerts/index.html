Lock-screen alerts

Know the moment someone asks.

This phone gets a notification whenever someone requests a song through your booth link, even when it's locked in your pocket. Tap it to play the song or turn it down.

  1. Tap Share, the square with an arrow. In Chrome it's beside the address bar; in Safari it's at the bottom, or under ···.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen (scroll down if it isn't showing), then Add.
  3. Open DJ Alerts from your Home Screen and tap Turn on alerts there.

iPhone only lets an app on your Home Screen show notifications, so this is a one-time step.
